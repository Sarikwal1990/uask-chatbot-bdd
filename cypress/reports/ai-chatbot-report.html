How to get an Emirates ID? Step-by-Step Guide: How to Get an Emirates ID Obtaining an Emirates ID is a mandatory process for all UAE citizens and residents. The Emirates ID is your official identification and is required for accessing government services, opening bank accounts, signing contracts, and more. Below is a clear, step-by-step guide to help you through the process: 1. Ensure You Have a Valid UAE Residence Visa You must have a valid UAE residence visa before you can apply for an Emirates ID. This is a strict requirement for expatriate residents2. 2. Prepare the Required Documents The documents you need may vary depending on your nationality and age, but generally include: Original passport Valid UAE residence visa (for expatriates) Birth certificate (for children) Passport-sized photographs Supporting documents (e.g., marriage certificate, if applicable) Ensure all documents are original and valid. Photocopies may be accepted if attested by a notary public4. 3. Complete the Application You have two main options: Online: Visit the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Ports Security (ICP) website or use the ICP mobile app to fill out the application form123. Typing Centre: Visit an accredited typing centre, where staff will help you complete the application23. 4. Pay the Application Fee Pay the required fee online or at the typing centre. The fee depends on the validity period of your visa and the type of application (new, renewal, or replacement)2. 5. Biometric Data Collection First-time applicants (and some renewals) must visit an ICP service centre to provide biometric data, including fingerprints and a photograph23. You will be notified of your appointment after submitting your application. 6. Track Your Application Use your application number to track the status of your Emirates ID on the ICP website or app13. 7. Collect Your Emirates ID Once your application is approved, you will receive an SMS notification. Collect your Emirates ID from the designated Emirates Post office, or it may be delivered to your registered address23. You must collect your card within 90 days of notification3. Additional Notes If you need your Emirates ID urgently, the ICP offers a “Fawri” express service for UAE and GCC nationals (and for expatriates in case of replacement), with an additional fee1. Always carry your Emirates ID with you, as it is required for many official transactions and services12. If your Emirates ID is lost, stolen, or damaged, report it immediately and apply for a replacement1.
