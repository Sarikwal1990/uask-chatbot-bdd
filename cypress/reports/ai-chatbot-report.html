Submit Your Application You have two main options: Online: Apply through the ICP website or mobile app. Typing Centre: Visit an accredited typing centre, where staff will help you fill out the application form and collect your documents124. 4. Pay the Application Fee Fees vary depending on the validity period and applicant category. Payment can be made online or at the typing centre. 5. Biometric Data Collection After submitting your application, you will receive an appointment to visit an ICP service centre. At the centre, your fingerprints and photograph will be taken for biometric verification2. 6. Application Processing Once your biometrics are collected, your application will be processed. You will receive SMS notifications about the status of your application2. 7. Collect Your Emirates ID When your Emirates ID is ready, you will receive a notification. Collect your card from the designated Emirates Post office, or it may be delivered to your registered address2. 8. Track Your Application (Optional) You can track the status of your Emirates ID application online via the ICP website or through the typing centre4. 9. Urgent Service (Optional) The ICP offers a “Fawri” urgent service for UAE and GCC nationals, issuing the Emirates ID within 24 hours for an additional fee. Expatriates can use this only for card replacement, not for new issuance1. Important Notes Carry your Emirates ID at all times; it is a legal requirement. Report any loss, theft, or damage of your Emirates ID immediately to the ICP and apply for a replacement1. Update your details with the ICP if there are any changes (e.g., address, phone number) within one month1.
