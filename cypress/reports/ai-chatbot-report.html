Submit Your Application There are two main ways to apply: Online: Through the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Ports Security (ICP) website or mobile app. In Person: At an accredited typing centre, where staff will help you fill out the application form and collect your documents13. 4. Biometric Data Collection After submitting your application, you will be notified to visit an ICP service centre for biometric data collection (fingerprints and photograph).
