How to get an Emirates ID? Step-by-Step Guide: How to Get an Emirates ID The Emirates ID is a mandatory identification card for all UAE citizens and residents. It is essential for accessing government services, opening bank accounts, signing contracts, and many other daily activities in the UAE. Below is a clear, step-by-step guide to obtaining your Emirates ID, based on the latest official and expert sources. 1. Ensure You Have a Valid UAE Residence Visa You must have a valid UAE residence visa before applying for an Emirates ID. This is a strict requirement for expatriates and residents2. 2. Gather Required Documents The documents you need depend on your status: Applicant Type Required Documents UAE Nationals Passport, Family Book (Khulasat Al Qaid), Birth Certificate (for first-time applicants), Photo GCC Nationals Valid Passport, GCC ID Card, Proof of Residency, Photo Expatriate Residents Valid Passport with Residence Permit, Proof of Residence (e.g., tenancy contract, utility bill), Photo Children (<15 yrs) Birth Certificate, Passports of both parents, Child’s Photo All documents should be original and valid35. 3. Choose Your Application Method You can apply for an Emirates ID in one of the following ways: Online: Through the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Ports Security (ICP) website or mobile app. Typing Centre: Visit an accredited typing centre or Amer Centre, where staff will assist you with the application form1234. Customer Happiness Centre: For urgent or special cases, visit an ICP Customer Happiness Centre directly. 4. Complete the Application Form Fill out the Emirates ID application form with accurate information. If applying at a typing centre, staff will assist you. If applying online, follow the instructions on the ICP website or app124. 5. Pay the Application Fees Fees vary depending on the validity period (typically 1, 2, or 3 years). Payment can be made online or at the typing centre. Additional fees apply for urgent processing (Fawri service)13. 6. Provide Biometric Data First-time applicants (and sometimes renewals) must visit an ICP service centre to provide fingerprints and have a photograph taken. You will receive an appointment or instructions after submitting your application124. 7. Track Your Application Use your application number to track the status online via the ICP website or app. You will receive SMS notifications about your application progress and collection details124. 8. Collect Your Emirates ID Once approved, you will be notified by SMS. Collect your Emirates ID from the designated Emirates Post office or as instructed in your notification. You must collect the card within 90 days of notification4. 9. Special Notes For urgent issuance (within 24 hours), use the Fawri service at ICP Customer Happiness Centres (available for UAE and GCC nationals; expatriates only for replacement cases)1. Always carry your Emirates ID; it is illegal for others (including employers) to retain your card14. Report any loss, theft, or damage to ICP immediately and apply for a replacement1.
